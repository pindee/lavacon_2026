# Content

Apply these rules to every label, message, and heading you generate. They are a short version of `standards/style-guide-rules.md`. Replace them with your own.

## Writing
- Use active voice and present tense.
- Keep sentences short.
- Write for a general audience. Avoid jargon.

## Buttons and links
- Start with a verb. Use two or three words.
- Use descriptive link text. Never "click here".

## Errors
- Say what happened and what to do next. Don't blame the user.

## Terms
- Follow `terms/terms.json`. For example, write "sign in", not "log in".
