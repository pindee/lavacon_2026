# House chili (fictional recipe, 10 servings)

A made-up recipe for this teaching example. Four signature steps are marked ★. Scale from this base.

## Ingredients (10 servings)

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ground beef | 2 lb | Cook to 160°F (71°C) |
| Yellow onions | 2 medium | ★ Diced and undercooked on purpose |
| Garlic | 6 cloves | ★ Pressed the night before |
| Whole peeled tomatoes | 28 oz can | ★ Diced by hand, not crushed |
| Dried ancho chilies | 3 | ★ Toasted, stemmed, seeded, and soaked |
| Kidney beans | 2 cans (15 oz) | Drained |
| Beef broth | 2 cups | |
| Chili powder | 3 tbsp | |
| Ground cumin | 1 tbsp | |
| Salt | To taste | A person decides |

## Steps

**Night before**
1. ★ Press the garlic, cover it, and refrigerate.
2. ★ Toast the ancho chilies in a dry pan until fragrant. Stem and seed them, soak in hot water for 30 minutes, blend to a paste, and refrigerate.
3. ★ Dice the whole tomatoes by hand and refrigerate them in their juice.

**Day of**
4. Brown the beef until it reaches 160°F (71°C). Check with a thermometer.
5. ★ Cook the onions in a separate pan for 3 to 4 minutes. They should be softened but still crisp.
6. Combine beef, onions, garlic, tomatoes, ancho paste, chili powder, cumin, and broth.
7. Simmer for 90 minutes, stirring now and then.
8. Add the beans for the last 20 minutes.
9. Taste and adjust the seasoning. This is a human decision.
10. Hold at 140°F (60°C) or hotter until served.

## Food safety (general guidance, check your local rules)
- Cook ground beef to 160°F (71°C).
- Keep hot food at 140°F (60°C) or hotter.
- Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. If the room is above 90°F (32°C), within 1 hour.
- Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C).
- Don't leave chili out overnight.

## Transport
Use a lidded container and a cart, or two people. Don't carry a full pot alone.
