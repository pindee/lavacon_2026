# Example

## Request
"Plan chili for 20 people on Friday at noon. No allergies. We have a big pot and it has to go up two floors."

## Reply

**1. Summary**
20 servings (recipe doubled), served Friday at 12:00. Start the night before, on Thursday at 7:00 pm.

**2. Shopping list**

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ground beef | 4 lb | |
| Yellow onions | 4 medium | |
| Garlic | 12 cloves | Check you have at least 2 heads |
| Whole peeled tomatoes | 2 cans (28 oz) | |
| Dried ancho chilies | 6 | |
| Kidney beans | 4 cans (15 oz) | |
| Beef broth | 4 cups | |
| Chili powder | 6 tbsp | |
| Ground cumin | 2 tbsp | |
| Salt | To taste | |

**3. Timeline**

| When | Step | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Thu 7:00 pm | Press 12 garlic cloves, cover, refrigerate | You |
| Thu 7:30 pm | Toast, stem, seed, and soak 6 ancho chilies. Blend to a paste. Refrigerate | You |
| Thu 8:00 pm | Dice the whole tomatoes by hand. Refrigerate in their juice | You |
| Fri 9:00 am | Brown the beef until it reaches 160°F (71°C). Use a thermometer | You |
| Fri 9:30 am | Cook the onions 3 to 4 minutes in a separate pan, still crisp | You |
| Fri 9:45 am | Combine everything except the beans. Start a 90-minute simmer | You |
| Fri 10:55 am | Add the beans | You |
| Fri 11:15 am | Taste and adjust the seasoning | You (a person decides) |
| Fri 11:30 am | Move the pot in its lidded container on a cart, or with two people | You, plus a helper |
| Fri 11:45 am | Set up on warming equipment at 140°F (60°C) or hotter | You |
| Fri 12:00 pm | Serve | You |
| Fri by 2:00 pm | Refrigerate leftovers | You |

**4. Handoffs and safety notes**
- Human: all cooking, tasting, and seasoning. I can't do any of it.
- Human: lifting the pot. Two floors means the cart, plus a second person for the stairs or lift.
- Keep chili at 140°F (60°C) or hotter until it is served. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C).
- You said no allergies. I can't clear anyone, so check with guests before serving.

**5. Open questions**
None.
